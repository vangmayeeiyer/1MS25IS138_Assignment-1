ScholarPulse – AI Research Assistant

Overview

ScholarPulse is an AI-powered research assistant designed to help students and researchers organize and explore academic research.

Features

1. Topic Hub – Explore and organize research topics.
2. Research Questions – Generate potential research questions.
3. Keyword Generator – Suggest relevant academic keywords.
4. Paper Summarizer – Summarize academic research papers.
5. Research Gap Analyzer – Identify potential research gaps for further investigation.
6. Manuscript Outline Generator – Create structured research manuscript outlines.

Technologies Used

- Google AI Studio
- React
- TypeScript
- Node.js
- Gemini API
- Europe PMC REST API

Literature Integration

ScholarPulse uses Europe PMC to search academic publications and retrieve publication information for research exploration.

Important Note

AI-generated research suggestions should be independently checked against original academic publications before academic use.
